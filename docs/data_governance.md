# Phase I data governance status

This is the canonical, version-controlled statement of project governance
status. Territorial wording and record-level defaults are defined in
`src/constants.py`; other documents should link here rather than duplicate it.

Phase I uses public federal data describing He Sapa. It implements a provenance
and governance scaffold informed by CARE, FAIR, Local Contexts, OCAP®, and IEEE
2890-2025. It does **not** claim independent certification or authorization by
those organizations. The generic TK Notice is a notice of Indigenous interests,
not a Nation-authorized TK or BC Label.

## Current controls

- Territorial status, source, steward, and license fields are attached to
  published records.
- Public-source and generated artifacts are separated from future restricted
  data tiers.
- Release validation rejects incomplete provenance or degenerate commodity
  classification.

## Open prerequisites for Phase II

1. Formal presentation and review by relevant Lakota governance bodies.
2. Data-sharing agreements with participating Nations.
3. A co-designed ethics and intake review process.
4. Nation-specific Local Contexts label authorization.
5. A custody-transfer plan to Lakota-controlled infrastructure.
6. Tested restricted-access controls and incident procedures.
7. An authorized oral-history and sensitive-location protocol.

No sensitive Tribal data may enter this repository until these prerequisites
are satisfied. Public presentations should describe a "Phase I governance
scaffold with institutional prerequisites pending."
